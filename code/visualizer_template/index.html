Families of manuscripts

For two manuscripts, the overlap is the number of repeating illustration groups they share, divided by the number of groups found in either of them. Manuscripts and families are joined step by step, most similar first, while their average overlap stays above the chosen level. A family is a candidate for a shared visual language or a common model.
